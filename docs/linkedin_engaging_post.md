Most e-commerce dashboards can tell you what happened last week.

Almost none can tell you how confident you should be about what happens next.

I built CommerceLens to close that gap — a full analytics product sitting on top of a simulated 60,000-session marketplace, designed to keep asking the harder question underneath every metric: not just "what's the number," but "what's the probability, and what should you actually do about it?"

A few things you can do inside it:

→ Pick any two customer behaviors — cart activity, device, discount exposure, return history — and get the real conditional probability between them, computed live, not pulled from a lookup table.

→ Run an actual Bayes' theorem calculator on a real purchase-funnel question, and watch two completely independent ways of computing the same probability agree.

→ Simulate 500 future sessions and see the full spread of likely outcomes — not one "expected" number, the whole distribution.

→ Sample a campaign list without replacement and find out exactly how likely you are to land enough high-value customers, and why the obvious shortcut formula quietly gets it wrong.

→ Drop into a live data explorer and slice 60,000 sessions by device, income, channel, or segment in real time, server-side.

There's also a section built specifically to demonstrate how the exact same numbers can be charted two ways to tell two completely different stories — and how to catch it when someone does that to you.

Every chart is tied to an actual business question. Every number on the site is computed live — nothing is hard-coded. And every claim about what's associated with what is honest about the difference between correlation and causation, which is rarer than it should be in analytics work.

Try to break it. Drag the sliders to the edges. Pick the strangest combination of filters you can think of. It's built to hold up.
