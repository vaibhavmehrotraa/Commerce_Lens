Most analytics projects stop at a dashboard. I wanted one that treated customer behavior as a probability problem instead.

CommerceLens: 60,000 synthetic e-commerce sessions, run through descriptive statistics, conditional probability, Bayes' theorem, correlation, random variables, and Binomial/Hypergeometric/Exponential modeling — every number computed live, every chart tied to a real business question, every claim about association explicitly not a claim about causation.

Dataset is synthetic and reproducible by design — the point is demonstrating the analytical reasoning end to end, not reporting a real-world finding.

Built to bridge the gap between learning statistics and using statistics to make business decisions.
